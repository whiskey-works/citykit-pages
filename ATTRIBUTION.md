Griffin's map data comes from **OpenStreetMap**: © OpenStreetMap contributors. It is available under the
[Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). See

| Layer | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Streets, main roads, rail, water | [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) | ODbL 1.0, © OpenStreetMap contributors |
| Ground elevation (bare-earth DEM) | [USGS 3D Elevation Program](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program) | Public domain |
| Building heights (calibration) | USGS 3DEP lidar | Public domain |
| Buildings, lots, trees | Generated by CityKit on the data above | Part of the derived database: ODbL 1.0 |

The derived data this site serves, everything under `site/data/griffin/`, is offered under the ODbL. The full text
and its scope are in [LICENSE-DATA](LICENSE-DATA). The demo shows "© OpenStreetMap contributors · Elevation and
lidar: USGS 3DEP" on screen at all times, and the landing page's screenshot carries the same line.
